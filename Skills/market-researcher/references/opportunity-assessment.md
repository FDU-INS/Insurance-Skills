# Opportunity Assessment

## Opportunity Scoring Framework

Use this when researching business ideas, market gaps, or investment opportunities.

### Quick Viability Score

Rate each opportunity on these 8 dimensions (1-10 scale):

| Dimension | Score | Evidence |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| **Market Size** | /10 | [TAM/SAM data] |
| **Growth Rate** | /10 | [CAGR or trend data] |
| **Competition Level** | /10 | [10 = low competition] |
| **Barrier to Entry** | /10 | [10 = low barrier] |
| **Revenue Potential** | /10 | [unit economics data] |
| **Customer Pain Severity** | /10 | [how urgent is the problem?] |
| **Timing** | /10 | [market readiness] |
| **Feasibility** | /10 | [technical/operational viability] |
| **TOTAL** | /80 | |

**Score interpretation:**
- 65-80: Strong opportunity — pursue aggressively
- 50-64: Promising — worth deeper investigation
- 35-49: Marginal — significant risks or limitations
- Below 35: Weak — likely not worth pursuing

## Idea Generation Research

When user asks for "business ideas in [space]":

### Discovery Process

1. **Map the landscape**: Identify all sub-segments within the space
2. **Find pain points**: Search for complaints, unmet needs, workarounds
3. **Spot gaps**: Where existing solutions fall short
4. **Check timing**: What's changed recently that creates new opportunity?
5. **Validate demand**: Evidence that people would pay for a solution

## Due Diligence Checklist

When researching a specific opportunity in depth:

- [ ] Market size verified from 2+ sources
- [ ] Growth rate confirmed with recent data (last 12 months)
- [ ] Top 5 competitors identified with revenue/funding data
- [ ] Customer pain validated (reviews, forums, surveys)
- [ ] Unit economics benchmarked against comparables
- [ ] Regulatory requirements checked
- [ ] Technology feasibility assessed
- [ ] Key risks identified and probability-assessed
- [ ] Timing argument supported with evidence
- [ ] At least 3 "why this could fail" scenarios documented
