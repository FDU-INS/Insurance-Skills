# Protection Provider Comparison Guide

## Key Providers (UK Market)

### AIG Life
- **Strengths**: Competitive pricing, strong CI definitions, good service
- **CI Coverage**: Comprehensive (50+ conditions)
- **Claims Paid**: 95%+
- **Unique Features**: Additional payment conditions, children's cover included
- **Best For**: Competitive pricing with comprehensive cover

### Aviva
- **Strengths**: Large provider, comprehensive features, strong brand
- **CI Coverage**: Very comprehensive (60+ conditions), partial payments
- **Claims Paid**: 93-95%
- **Unique Features**: Fracture cover, additional payment benefits, rehab support
- **Best For**: Comprehensive protection, trusted brand

### Legal & General
- **Strengths**: Market-leading claims statistics, simple products
- **CI Coverage**: Good (50+ conditions)
- **Claims Paid**: 97%+ (industry leading)
- **Unique Features**: Straightforward products, excellent claims philosophy
- **Best For**: Claims certainty, straightforward needs

### LV=

- **CI Coverage**: Excellent (50+ conditions), low severity thresholds
- **Claims Paid**: 94-96%
- **Unique Features**: Budget Boost (premium back), flexible trust options
- **Best For**: Value with comprehensive cover

### Royal London
- **Strengths**: Mutual, strong heritage, comprehensive cover
- **CI Coverage**: Very good (55+ conditions)
- **Claims Paid**: 95%+
- **Unique Features**: Mutual benefits, strong CI definitions
- **Best For**: Long-term security, mutual structure

### Scottish Provident (part of Royal London)
- **Strengths**: Specialist protection provider, strong IP focus
- **CI Coverage**: Good
- **IP**: Industry-leading own occupation definitions
- **Claims Paid**: 95%+
- **Unique Features**: Excellent IP product, strong underwriting
- **Best For**: Income protection, own occupation definitions

### The Exeter
- **Strengths**: Specialist IP provider, excellent definitions
- **IP**: Market-leading own occupation, comprehensive features
- **Claims Paid**: Very high
- **Unique Features**: Best-in-class IP definitions, rehabilitation focus
- **Best For**: Income protection (especially professionals)

### Vitality
- **Strengths**: Health and wellness focus, premium discounts
- **CI Coverage**: Comprehensive (50+ conditions)
- **Claims Paid**: 94%+
- **Unique Features**: Healthy living rewards (up to 30% discount), gym membership

### Zurich
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive cover, strong features
- **CI Coverage**: Very comprehensive (65+ conditions)
- **Claims Paid**: 94-96%
- **Unique Features**: Wide conditions list, back-to-work guarantee
- **Best For**: Comprehensive protection, wide conditions coverage

## Comparison Factors

### 1. Premium Cost

**Not Always Cheapest is Best**:
- Check like-for-like (same sum assured, term, features)
- Consider definition quality
- Factor in claims statistics
- Review added benefits

**Price Variation**:
- Can vary 30-50% between providers
- Age and health impact pricing differently
- Smoker/non-smoker differentials vary

**Example (Male age 35, £250k, 25 years, level term)**:
- Cheapest: £12/month
- Most expensive: £18/month
- Difference: £50% (£1,800 over term)

**When to Pay More**:
- Better claims record
- Better CI definitions
- Added benefits valuable
- Stronger financial position

### 2. Claims Statistics

**What to Check**:
- % of claims paid (target 90%+)
- Average time to process claim
- Customer satisfaction ratings
- Complaints ratio

**Top Performers** (Claims Paid %):
- Legal & General: 97%+
- Royal London: 96%+
- Aviva: 95%+
- AIG: 95%+

**Why Claims Declined**:
- Non-disclosure (most common)
- Doesn't meet policy definition
- Exclusions apply
- Outside policy terms

**Red Flag**: Provider with <85% claims paid

### 3. Critical Illness Definitions

**Key Differences**:
- Severity thresholds
- Number of conditions
- Partial payment conditions
- Exclusions

**Defaqto Ratings**:
- **5 Star**: Most comprehensive (Aviva, Zurich, LV=)
- **4 Star**: Good coverage (AIG, Legal & General)
- **3 Star**: Standard coverage

**Specific Definition Quality**:

**Cancer**:
- Best: Covers early-stage cancers (some)
- Standard: Excludes certain early cancers
- Check: Prostate cancer coverage (varies significantly)

**Heart Attack**:
- Best: 2 out of 3 diagnostic criteria
- Standard: All 3 criteria required
- Check: Troponin levels required

**Stroke**:
- Best: Permanent symptoms (any severity)
- Standard: Permanent and significant
- Check: TIA coverage (rarely covered)

### 4. Income Protection Definitions

**Own Occupation** (Best):
- Scottish Provident: Excellent
- The Exeter: Market-leading
- Royal London: Very good
- Aviva: Good

**Suited Occupation** (Mid):
- Some providers use this
- Less clear, more disputes
- Avoid if possible

**Any Occupation** (Poor):
- Budget providers
- Very hard to claim
- Only if no alternative

**Mental Health Coverage**:
- Best: No limitations (full cover)
- Standard: 12-24 month limit
- Poor: Excluded entirely

**Check**:
- Is own occupation available for client's job?
- Are there mental health limitations?
- What are proportionate benefit terms?

### 5. Added Benefits and Features

**Rehabilitation Benefits**:
- Aviva: £25,000 rehab benefit
- Zurich: Back-to-work guarantee
- The Exeter: Extensive rehab support

**Children's Cover**:
- Aviva: Comprehensive children's CI
- AIG: Strong children's cover
- Most others: Basic children's cover included

**Additional Payment Conditions**:
- Aviva: Fracture cover, other partial payments
- AIG: Additional payment benefits
- LV=: Budget Boost (premium back feature)

**Virtual GP/Health Services**:
- Aviva: Digital GP
- Vitality: Wellness program, gym discounts
- AIG: Second medical opinion

### 6. Financial Strength Ratings

**Rating Agencies**:
- Standard & Poor's (S&P)
- Moody's
- Fitch

**Ratings to Look For**:
- AAA/AA: Excellent
- A: Very good
- BBB: Good
- Below BBB: Caution

**Why It Matters**:
- Will they pay claim in 20-30 years?
- Financial stability
- Ability to honor commitments

**Top Rated**:
- Legal & General: AA+
- Aviva: A+
- Zurich: AA-
- Most major providers: A or better

### 7. Application and Service

**Application Process**:
- Online application available?
- Speed of underwriting decisions
- Quality of support during application

**Ongoing Service**:
- Online portal access
- Ease of making changes
- Customer service quality
- Claims process ease

**Customer Satisfaction**:
- Defaqto Service Ratings
- Trustpilot scores
- FCA complaints data

## Comparison Checklist

When comparing providers, check:

### Life Insurance
- [ ] Premium cost (like-for-like)
- [ ] Claims statistics (target 90%+)
- [ ] Financial strength rating (A or better)
- [ ] Terminal illness inclusion (most include free)
- [ ] Trust facility (free)
- [ ] Guaranteed premiums
- [ ] Conversion/renewal options

### Critical Illness
- [ ] Premium cost
- [ ] Defaqto rating (5-star ideal)
- [ ] Number of conditions covered (50+)
- [ ] Cancer definition (covers early-stage?)
- [ ] Heart attack definition (2/3 criteria or 3/3?)
- [ ] Stroke definition (severity threshold)
- [ ] Children's cover included
- [ ] Partial payment conditions
- [ ] Additional benefits (rehab, etc.)
- [ ] Claims statistics

### Income Protection
- [ ] Premium cost
- [ ] Definition of incapacity (own occupation ideal)
- [ ] Mental health coverage (unlimited ideal)
- [ ] Proportionate benefit (part return to work)
- [ ] Rehabilitation support
- [ ] Deferred period options
- [ ] Benefit period options (to retirement ideal)
- [ ] Guaranteed premiums
- [ ] Waiver of premium inclusion

## Provider Selection Strategy

### Step 1: Identify Client Priorities

**Questions**:
- Lowest cost most important?
- Best definitions most important?
- Claims certainty most important?
- Specific features needed?

### Step 2: Shortlist Providers

**Based on**:
- Product type (life, CI, IP)
- Client priorities
- Known strengths
- Typical pricing for client profile

### Step 3: Obtain Quotes

**Compare**:
- 3-5 providers minimum
- Like-for-like terms
- Same sum assured, term, features

### Step 4: Quality Assessment

**Beyond Price**:
- Check Defaqto ratings
- Review claims statistics
- Compare definitions (CI/IP)
- Check added benefits

### Step 5: Recommendation

**Document**:
- Why this provider chosen
- What alternatives considered
- Why alternatives rejected
- Key benefits of choice
- Any trade-offs (e.g., cost vs features)

## Common Provider Myths

**Myth: "All CI policies are the same"**
- False: Definitions vary significantly
- Impact: Some much harder to claim

**Myth: "Cheapest is best"**
- False: Price vs value trade-off
- Impact: May pay less but get less

**Myth: "Big brands always better"**
- False: Specialists often have better products
- Impact: The Exeter (IP) better than some big brands

**Myth: "Claims statistics don't matter"**
- False: 97% vs 85% is huge difference
- Impact: 15% more likely to have claim declined

**Myth: "Once declined, can't get insurance"**
- False: Try different providers, different products
- Impact: Give up too easily

## Red Flags

**Avoid Providers With**:
- Claims paid <85%
- Financial strength rating below A
- Very cheap premiums (too good to be true)
- Poor FCA complaints record
- No Defaqto rating
- No clear policy documents

**Warning Signs**:
- Difficult application process
- Poor customer service during application
- Unclear policy terms
- Hidden exclusions
- Pressure to apply quickly

---

## Best Practice

**Good Provider Comparison Includes**:
- Minimum 3 providers quoted
- Like-for-like comparison (same terms)
- Defaqto ratings checked (CI/IP)
- Claims statistics reviewed
- Financial strength confirmed
- Added benefits compared
- Recommendation justified
- Alternatives documented (why rejected)
- Client's priorities met
- Best value demonstrated (not just price)

**Document in Suitability Letter**:
- Providers considered (at least 3)
- Comparison factors used
- Why recommended provider chosen
- What client is gaining (better definitions/claims/features)
- What client might be giving up (if paying more, what for?)
