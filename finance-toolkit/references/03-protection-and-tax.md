# Insurance & Tax Optimization for Vietnam

## Insurance: Minimum Viable Plan

### Coverage Tiers

| Priority | Coverage | Why Essential | Approximate Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0** | BHYT (social health) | Legally required; public hospital access | ~1–2% of salary (employer/employee shared) |
| **P0** | Motorbike insurance (Bảo hiểm bắt buộc) | Legally required | ~120–200k VND/year |
| **P1** | Private inpatient health | International/private hospital access | 3–8M VND/year/person |
| **P1** | Critical illness | Protects savings from catastrophic diagnosis | 3–6M VND/year/person |
| **P2** | Term life | Required if: shared mortgage, dependents | 2–5M VND/year (varies by coverage) |
| **P3** | Outpatient add-on | Convenience; not urgent | 2–4M VND/year add-on |

### BHYT — Bảo Hiểm Y Tế (Social Health Insurance)

**What it covers:**
- Public hospital inpatient care: 80–100% of approved costs
- Outpatient at registered clinic: 80% of approved costs
- Prescription drugs: 50–100% depending on category
- Emergency care: covered regardless of registered hospital

**What it does NOT cover:**
- Private/international hospitals (Vinmec, FV Hospital, Columbia Asia)
- Dental (beyond basic extractions)
- Cosmetic procedures
- Long waiting times and basic room conditions (shared wards)

**How it works:** Employer deducts automatically. Employee pays 1.5%, employer pays 3% of salary.

**Registration:** Tied to your BHXH number. Register at your district health center for your "base" outpatient clinic.

### Private Health Insurance

**Why get it:** Eliminates the "comfort gap" — access to international hospitals with English-speaking doctors, single rooms, and shorter wait times.

**Key insurers in Vietnam:**
| Insurer | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bảo Việt | Local; widely accepted | Good network; competitive pricing |
| PVI | Local; strong for inpatient | Popular with corporate plans |
| AXA / Manulife / Prudential | International; comprehensive | Higher premiums; better for expat-tier hospitals |
| BHXH supplemental plans | Government-linked | Basic top-up; very affordable |

**What to look for:**
- Inpatient coverage ≥ 200M VND/year per person
- Pre-existing condition waiting period (typically 12–24 months)
- Direct billing hospitals list (so you don't pay out of pocket first)
- Cashless admission at Vinmec, FV, Columbia Asia

**Cost guidance (age 25, healthy):** 3–8M VND/year for basic inpatient plan; 8–15M for comprehensive inpatient + outpatient

### Life Insurance: Term vs. Whole Life

**Term life insurance:**
- Pure death benefit for a fixed period (10, 20, 30 years)
- Significantly cheaper than whole life
- Right choice for most mid-20s couples
- Get it when: shared mortgage, dependents (children, elderly parents relying on income)
- Coverage amount: 5–10× annual income (so 200–400M for someone earning 20–40M/month)
- Cost: 2–5M VND/year for a healthy 25-year-old for 500M VND coverage

**Whole life insurance:**
- Combines insurance with savings/investment component
- Significantly higher premiums; lower investment returns vs. standalone investments
- Often sold aggressively by agents as "investment"
- **Not recommended** before emergency fund is complete and term coverage in place

**Investment-linked insurance (ILP / Bảo hiểm liên kết đầu tư):**
- High fees (2–4% annual management fee + front load)
- Low surrender value in early years
- **Avoid** until core investment portfolio is established

### Critical Illness Insurance

- Pays lump sum on diagnosis of major illness (cancer, stroke, heart attack)
- This lump sum covers: lost income, treatment not covered by health insurance, lifestyle adjustments
- Typically covers 20–50 illnesses
- Cost: 3–6M VND/year for 500M–1B VND coverage at age 25
- **Do not skip** — this is the coverage gap that destroys young families' finances

### Motorbike Insurance

- **Bảo hiểm trách nhiệm dân sự bắt buộc**: legally required, covers third-party liability
- Cost: ~120–200k VND/year (almost free)
- Buy when renewing vehicle registration (đăng kiểm) or at any insurer
- **Voluntary comprehensive coverage**: 500k–2M VND/year; covers your own bike damage/theft — worth it for bikes 50M+ VND

### Common Insurance Gaps (Young Couples)
1. BHYT only → relies on public hospitals; no private hospital access
2. Zero life insurance → if one income disappears, shared expenses collapse
3. No critical illness → one cancer diagnosis can wipe 5–10 years of savings
4. Forgotten motorbike renewal → riding uninsured is both illegal and financially exposed
5. Bought VUL/ILP before emergency fund → locked into high-fee product, no liquidity

## Personal Income Tax (PIT) — Thuế Thu Nhập Cá Nhân

### Key Facts
- Vietnam taxes individuals, NOT households — no joint filing for married couples
- Each spouse files/is assessed independently
- Tax year = calendar year (January 1 – December 31)

### Standard Deductions (2024–2025 rates)

| Deduction | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Personal deduction | 11,000,000 VND/month | Each taxpayer receives this |
| Dependent deduction | 4,400,000 VND/month per dependent | Children under 18, or disabled; elderly parents who meet income test |
| Compulsory insurance | Full BHXH + BHYT + BHTN contribution | ~10.5% of salary; automatically deducted |

**Dependent registration:** Apply at your local tax office (Chi cục Thuế) with supporting documents (birth certificate for children; family register for parents). Only ONE spouse can claim a given dependent.

### PIT Brackets (On Taxable Income = Gross − Deductions)

| Taxable Income (monthly) | Tax Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to 5,000,000 VND | 5% |
| 5,000,001 – 10,000,000 | 10% |
| 10,000,001 – 18,000,000 | 15% |
| 18,000,001 – 32,000,000 | 20% |
| 32,000,001 – 52,000,000 | 25% |
| 52,000,001 – 80,000,000 | 30% |
| Over 80,000,000 | 35% |

### Worked Example (Single Person, Gross 25M VND/month)

```
Gross salary:                          25,000,000
- Personal deduction:                 -11,000,000
- BHXH/BHYT/BHTN (10.5%):             -2,625,000
= Taxable income:                      11,375,000

Tax calculation:
  First 5M @ 5%:                          250,000
  Next 5M @ 10%:                          500,000
  Remaining 1.375M @ 15%:                 206,250
= Total monthly PIT:                      956,250
= ~3.8% effective tax rate
```

### Dual-Income Couple Tax Optimization

**Strategy 1: Register all eligible dependents**
- Each elderly parent who earns < 1M VND/month qualifies as a dependent
- Claim deduction for the parent on the spouse with the HIGHER income (saves more tax)
- Children: claim on the higher-income spouse

**Strategy 2: Maximize non-taxable employer allowances**
These are NOT subject to PIT if within legal limits:
| Allowance | Tax-free limit |
|---|---|
| Meal allowance | 730,000 VND/month |
| Phone allowance | Up to actual cost (with receipts) |
| Uniform/protective clothing | Per regulation |
| Business travel/reimbursement | With supporting receipts |

**Strategy 3: Ensure BHXH on full actual salary**
- Some employers pay "split salary" (formal + informal) — informal portion not contributing to BHXH
- This reduces BHXH deductions, making more income taxable AND reducing future pension benefit
- Better to have full BHXH contributions on actual salary

**Strategy 4: Timing bonuses**
- If a large bonus pushes you into a higher bracket, check if deferring to January (new tax year) is possible

### Tax Filing Obligations

**Single employer, single income:**
- Employer does monthly provisional withholding (khấu trừ tại nguồn)
- Year-end tax finalization (quyết toán thuế) done by employer
- Employee gets refund or pays balance in Q1 of following year

**Multiple income sources (e.g., freelance + salary, two employers):**
- Must personally file tax finalization by **March 31** of following year
- Online filing at: thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn
- Need: income statements from all employers (chứng từ khấu trừ thuế)

**Self-employed / freelance only:**
- Register as individual business or file as freelancer
- Declare quarterly; finalize by March 31

### Social Insurance Quick Reference

| Insurance | Employee Pays | Employer Pays | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| BHXH (social) | 8% | 17.5% | Pension, sickness leave, maternity |
| BHYT (health) | 1.5% | 3% | Health coverage |
| BHTN (unemployment) | 1% | 1% | Unemployment benefit |
| **Total** | **10.5%** | **21.5%** | |

Cap: contributions capped at 20× minimum wage base (= max ~4,729,400 VND/month employee contribution as of 2024)
