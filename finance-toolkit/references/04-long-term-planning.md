# Long-Term Planning: Retirement & Home Buying in HCMC

## Retirement Planning

### BHXH — Bảo Hiểm Xã Hội (Compulsory Social Insurance)

**How it works:**
- Accumulates pension points throughout your working life
- Pension at retirement = 45–75% of average salary (based on years of contribution)
- Minimum years to qualify for full pension: 20 years of BHXH contributions
- Current retirement ages: Men = 62 (by 2028), Women = 60 (by 2035)

**Maximizing your BHXH:**
1. **Ensure contributions on actual salary** — some employers pay minimum wage as BHXH base; negotiate to have BHXH on full salary
2. **Never withdraw BHXH early** (Rút BHXH một lần) unless truly necessary — you lose all accumulated pension rights; many young Vietnamese withdraw for short-term cash and regret it at retirement
3. **Continue contributing even between jobs** — you can pay voluntarily during gaps (voluntary BHXH: 22% of minimum wage/month)
4. **Keep record of your BHXH book** (sổ BHXH) — verify contributions are being made correctly at sổbhxh.vn

**Expected pension (rough estimate):**
- 30 years contribution, average salary 20M VND/month → pension ~9M VND/month (45%)
- 30 years contribution, average salary 30M VND/month → pension ~13.5M VND/month
- **This is not enough to maintain lifestyle** → voluntary top-up essential

### Voluntary Pension Funds (Quỹ Hưu Trí Tự Nguyện)

Vietnam introduced voluntary pension funds in 2021. Key features:
- Tax benefit: contributions up to 1M VND/month are tax-deductible (reduces PIT)
- Investment options: balanced funds (stocks + bonds), conservative (bonds), aggressive (mostly stocks)
- Long-term horizon (30–40 years) benefits enormously from compounding

**Available providers:**
| Fund | Manager | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| VLGF | Dragon Capital | Most established; solid track record |
| VCPF | VinaCapital | Strong asset management; competitive returns |
| Pension products | Manulife, Prudential | Insurance-wrapped; check fees carefully |

**Recommended approach:**
- Start with 1–2M VND/month into voluntary pension fund by age 27–30
- Choose a balanced or growth-oriented fund (long time horizon = can accept volatility)
- Treat it as untouchable until retirement (no early withdrawal except hardship)

### Retirement Savings Rate Target

| Age | BHXH only | With voluntary pension | Aggressive (FIRE target) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 10.5% compulsory | +3–5% voluntary | +15–20% voluntary |
| 30 | 10.5% compulsory | +5–8% voluntary | +20%+ voluntary |

**Rule of thumb:** Aim to replace 70–80% of pre-retirement income. BHXH alone provides ~45–75% but based on likely lower future average salaries. Top up the gap.

### Mortgage Requirements (Vietnamese Banks)

**Standard requirements:**
- **Down payment**: 20–30% of property value (most banks require 30%)
- **Loan-to-value (LTV)**: 70–80% maximum
- **Debt-to-income ratio**: Monthly repayment ≤ 40–50% of combined take-home pay
- **Loan term**: Up to 25–30 years (varies by bank and borrower age)
- **Age limit**: Loan must be fully repaid before age 65–70

**Key banks for home loans:**
| Bank | Notable Feature |
|---|---|
| Techcombank | Competitive rates; fast digital approval |
| VIB | Popular for flexible repayment terms |
| VPBank | Competitive variable rates |
| Vietcombank / VietinBank | Slightly lower rates but stricter criteria |
| BIDV | State bank; stable rates |

**Interest rate structure:**
- First 1–3 years: fixed "promotional rate" (typically 6–8% p.a.)
- After fixed period: floating rate (prime + margin, typically 10–14% p.a. total)
- Always model the floating rate scenario in your affordability calculation

### Affordability Calculator

**Simple check:**
```
Maximum home loan you can service:
= (Monthly take-home × 40%) ÷ mortgage payment factor

For 25-year loan at 10%: factor = ~0.00909 per VND borrowed per month
Example: Combined take-home 40M × 40% = 16M max monthly payment
Maximum loan = 16M ÷ 0.00909 = ~1.76B VND
With 30% down = 1.76B ÷ 70% = ~2.51B VND property price
```

### Legal Due Diligence (Critical)

**Only buy property with a clean title document:**
- **Sổ hồng** (Pink book / GCN QSHNƠ): Apartment/condo ownership certificate
- **Sổ đỏ** (Red book / GCNQSDD): Land use right certificate
- Verify at the district-level Land Registration Office (Văn phòng Đăng ký Đất đai)
- Check for: no mortgages/liens, no disputes, compliant construction permits

**Red flags:**
- "Hợp đồng góp vốn" (Capital contribution contract) instead of official sales contract
- Off-plan (hình thành trong tương lai) with developer of questionable financial health
- Missing sổ hồng 2+ years after handover

### Home-Buying Roadmap (Step by Step)

**Phase 1: Preparation (1–3 years before purchase)**
1. Set target property budget and location
2. Build 30% down payment + 5–10% for transaction costs (fees, taxes, furnishing)
3. Clean up CIC score — zero late payments for at least 12 months
4. Save documented income history — banks want 6–12 months of payslips
5. Estimate monthly mortgage payment and confirm it's < 40% of future take-home

**Phase 2: Search (3–6 months)**
6. Shortlist 5–10 properties within budget
7. Research developer reputation (for new builds) or building history (for secondary)
8. Get pre-approval letter from 2 banks to know your borrowing power

**Phase 3: Purchase**
9. Engage a reputable real estate lawyer for title verification (2–5M VND)
10. Sign sales contract (hợp đồng mua bán) — review every clause, especially penalty clauses
11. Pay 10% deposit; confirm refund conditions if loan is rejected
12. Submit mortgage application with all income documentation

**Phase 4: Post-purchase**
13. Register transfer with Notary Public (Công chứng) → 0.5% of value
14. Pay property transfer tax: 0.5% of declared value (buyer) + 2% personal income from property transfer (seller)
15. Apply for sổ hồng (expect 3–6 months at land registry)
16. Set up monthly mortgage auto-debit from joint account

### Transaction Costs (Buyer's Total Budget)

| Cost | Amount |
|---|---|
| Down payment | 30% of property price |
| Registration fee (lệ phí trước bạ) | 0.5% of declared value |
| Notary fee | 0.1–0.5% of value |
| Property agency commission | 1–2% (if using agent) |
| Furnishing/renovation | 50–300M VND |
| Mortgage processing fee | 0.5–1% of loan |
| **Total cash needed** | **~33–38% of property price** |
