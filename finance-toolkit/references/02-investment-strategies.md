# Vietnam Investment Strategies

## Investment Ladder (Priority Order for Beginners)

1. **Emergency fund** (3–6 months) — non-negotiable before any investing
2. **VN30 ETF or VN-Index stocks** — core long-term wealth engine
3. **Corporate bonds / term deposits** — stability layer
4. **Gold (SJC/PNJ)** — cultural hedge, 10–20% allocation
5. **International ETFs** — diversification beyond Vietnam
6. **Crypto** — speculative, 10% max, treat as high-risk

---

## Vietnamese Stock Market

### Exchanges & Indices
- **HoSE** (Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange): Main exchange; largest companies
- **HNX** (Hanoi Stock Exchange): Mid/small caps
- **VN-Index**: Broad market index (all HoSE stocks) — volatile, influenced by foreign flows
- **VN30**: Top 30 companies by market cap and liquidity — better for beginners (more stable)
- **VN30 ETF**: Passive fund tracking VN30; lowest cost entry into Vietnamese equities

### Top Trading Platforms for Young Investors

| Platform | Strengths | Commission | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TCBS** (Techcombank Securities) | Digital-first, iBond access, clean UX | 0.15–0.25% | Beginners; stocks + bonds in one place |
| **VPS** (Vietnam Prosperity Securities) | Low fees, fast execution, popular with active traders | 0.15% | Active traders, ETFs |
| **MBS** (MB Securities) | Strong research, MB Bank integration | 0.15–0.25% | Research-oriented investors |
| **SSI** | Largest broker, international ETF access | 0.15–0.25% | More advanced investors |
| **Mirae Asset** | International backing, margin accounts | 0.15% | Growth-oriented traders |

### Account Opening (All Platforms)
1. Download app → CMND/CCCD photo upload
2. eKYC verification (5–15 minutes)
3. Fund account via bank transfer
4. Start trading (T+2 settlement for stocks)

### Key Rules for VN Stock Market
- Foreign ownership limits apply to many sectors (banking: 30%, real estate: varies)
- Market hours: 9:00–11:30 AM, 1:00–3:00 PM weekdays (HoSE)
- Price movement capped at ±7% per session (HoSE)
- Dividends: often paid as stock dividends (cổ tức bằng cổ phiếu) not cash

## Gold (SJC & PNJ)

### Cultural Significance
Gold is a deeply embedded store of value in Vietnamese culture. Families buy gold for:
- Weddings (gold rings/jewelry as dowry, gifts)
- Tet holidays (gifting gold for luck)
- Emergency reserve ("physical insurance")

### SJC vs PNJ

| | SJC (Công ty Vàng bạc Đá quý Sài Gòn) | PNJ (Phú Nhuận Jewelry) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | State-mandated gold standard in Vietnam | Private retailer; widely trusted |
| Form | 1-tael bars (37.5g), coins | Jewelry, small bars |
| Premium over spot | Significant premium (state-controlled) | Lower premium, more competitive |
| Liquidity | High — accepted everywhere | High — 300+ stores nationwide |
| Best for | Long-term store of value | Smaller purchases, jewelry |

### Gold Allocation Strategy
- 10–20% of investment portfolio for a Vietnamese couple is culturally appropriate
- Buy physical: PNJ stores or SJC-licensed dealers
- Store securely: bank safe deposit box (0.5–2M VND/year) or home safe
- Avoid gold savings accounts at some banks (fees erode returns)

## Bonds

### Vietnamese Corporate Bonds (Trái phiếu doanh nghiệp)
- **Where to buy**: TCBS iBond platform (most accessible for retail), VPS Bond
- **Typical yields**: 8–12% p.a. for corporate bonds (vs 4–7% for term deposits)
- **Risk**: Corporate bonds are NOT insured (unlike bank deposits up to 125M VND via BHTG)
- **Due diligence**: Check issuer financial health; avoid highly leveraged real estate developers
- **Minimum**: Often 100M–500M VND per bond lot

### Government Bonds (Trái phiếu chính phủ)
- Safest fixed income in Vietnam
- Lower yield (~4–6%) but zero default risk
- Available via commercial banks or HNX

## Real Estate & REITs

### HCMC Property Market Reality
- **Entry-level investment property**: Minimum 1.5–3 billion VND (affordable districts)
- **Mid-range apartment**: 3–5 billion (Districts 7, Binh Thanh, Thu Duc)
- **Premium**: 5–15B+ (Districts 1, 2, 3)
- **Land**: Extremely high in HCMC; better in satellite provinces

### REITs in Vietnam
- Vietnam REIT market is nascent (regulations evolving as of 2024–2025)
- **FPTS REIT**, **Techcom REIT**: early-stage funds available
- Fractional real estate platforms emerging (proceed with caution — verify legal framework)
- **Alternative**: Buy stocks in real estate companies (Vinhomes, Novaland, Nam Long) for indirect RE exposure

### For Couples Not Ready to Buy
- Focus on building the 30% down payment first
- Park home savings in VN30 ETF or term deposits (not risky assets)
- REIT exposure can serve as a proxy while building capital

## International ETFs & Foreign Investments

### Accessible Routes for Vietnamese Investors
- **Via licensed brokers**: SSI, VPS offer access to some international ETFs
- **Futu Securities** (Moomoo): Available in Vietnam; access to US/HK stocks
- **Interactive Brokers**: Accessible to Vietnamese residents; full US market access
- Tax note: Foreign investment income technically taxable at 5% in Vietnam; consult a tax advisor

### Recommended International Exposure
- VOO / VTI (US total market) for global diversification
- Asia-Pacific ETFs for regional exposure without full EM risk
- Keep international to 5–15% of total portfolio — primary wealth engine should stay in VN for currency alignment with life goals (home in HCMC is priced in VND)

## Crypto

### Vietnam Context
- Vietnam has one of the world's highest crypto adoption rates (per Chainalysis)
- **Legal status**: Crypto is NOT legal as a payment method; trading/holding is in a grey zone
- No official crypto exchange regulated in Vietnam; most use Binance, Remitano
- P2P trading (crypto ↔ VND) via Remitano or Binance P2P is common

### Sensible Approach for Young Couple
- Limit to 5–10% of total portfolio
- Only after emergency fund AND initial stock/bond portfolio established
- BTC and ETH only (avoid altcoins for this portfolio tier)
- Use hardware wallet for anything > 50M VND in crypto
- Never invest borrowed money in crypto

## Sample Moderate Portfolio (Age 25, Combined Salary 50M VND/month)

Assuming 20% savings rate = 10M VND/month invested after emergency fund complete:

| Asset | Allocation | Monthly VND | Product |
|---|---|---|---|
| VN30 ETF | 50% | 5M | Via TCBS or VPS |
| Corporate bonds / term deposits | 25% | 2.5M | TCBS iBond or Techcombank deposit |
| Gold (SJC/PNJ) | 15% | 1.5M | Monthly purchase at PNJ; accumulate |
| International ETF / crypto | 10% | 1M | Moomoo (ETF) or Binance (crypto) |

**Rebalance annually.** Increase stock allocation as income grows and emergency fund stays intact.
